## Hiring Strategy

### Engineering (Pune/Indore/Jaipur)
- Lower cost than Mumbai/Bangalore
- Good talent pool from local colleges
- Remote-friendly culture
- Target: 70% of engineering team

### Customer-Facing (Mumbai)
- Sales and customer success in Mumbai (real estate hub)
- Close to customers for site visits and meetings
- Target: 100% of sales/CS team

### Fully Remote
- Open to remote engineers from anywhere in India
- Async-first culture (written communication)
- Quarterly in-person meets (Mumbai/Pune)

## Key Hires Priority

1. **Tech Lead**: Must have Node.js + K8s + security experience. Non-negotiable first hire.
2. **Full-Stack Engineers**: Must know React/TypeScript + Node.js. Prefer real-world SaaS experience.
3. **DevOps**: K8s, Terraform, AWS/GCP. Can start part-time.
4. **Customer Success**: Real estate domain knowledge preferred. Can be non-technical.
5. **ML Engineer**: NLP experience, LLM fine-tuning. Nice-to-have, not blocker.
